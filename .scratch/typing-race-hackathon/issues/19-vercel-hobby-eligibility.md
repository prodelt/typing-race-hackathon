# 19 Task: Confirm Vercel Hobby eligibility for a prize hackathon

Type: task
Status: closed (out of scope)
Blocked by: none
Mode: HITL (the agent drafts the support request; the user sends it from their Vercel account)

## Question

Vercel Hobby is for "personal or non-commercial use" only. Vercel defines "commercial" as financial gain of *anyone* involved in any part of producing the project, and it has no official ruling on hackathons with cash prizes (research 05, §8).

Does Vercel confirm in writing that this hackathon entry may be deployed on Hobby? The entry is free, has no ads or payments, and competes for a 30 000 UAH prize fund. Paying for Pro would conflict with TZ §7, which forbids requiring a paid service for the mandatory demo.

The agent drafts the English support request; the user sends it. The reply feeds the hosting choice in ticket "Stack & architecture":
- keep Vercel as the user wants, or
- promote a free static host from fallback to primary.

Until then, the build stays host-agnostic.

## Answer

Ruled out of scope on 2026-09-13. This is an internal company hackathon, and the user confirmed that deploying on Vercel Hobby is fine. No support request is needed, and hosting stays on Vercel Hobby.
